\## 7-Day South Indian Vegetarian Weight-Loss Meal Plan



\---



\# ROOT NODE — USER PROFILE \& OBJECTIVE



Read and lock the following user variables.



\[USER\_VARIABLES]



\- Calorie Goal: 1500 kcal / day

\- Macro Split: 40% Carbs, 30% Protein, 30% Fats

\- Activity Level: Sedentary (Desk job, 2-3 short walks a week)

\- Allergies/Intolerances: Cucumber seeds

\- Dislikes/Exclusions: Mushrooms, Bitter gourd (Pavakkai)

\- Cooking Time Limits: Maximum 30 minutes for breakfast/dinner, batch-cooks lunch on weekends

\- Region/Palate Preference: Tamil Nadu style / mild spice



\[/USER\_VARIABLES]



Treat these variables as hard constraints.



The objective is to create the most practical, culturally authentic and nutritionally balanced 7-day vegetarian weight-loss meal plan.



Do not expose internal reasoning or hidden chain-of-thought.

\- Idli

\- Dosa

\- Adai

\- Ragi

\- Sambar

\- Kootu

\- Poriyal

\- Sundal



while modifying portions to approach the calorie and macro targets.

\- Fat

\*\*1500 kcal/day\*\*

\- Carbohydrates: 40%

\- Protein: 30%

\- Fat: 30%

\- Fat ≈ 50 g/day

\# BRANCH 10 — REPAIR TREE



If a selected day is significantly outside the target, do not immediately discard the entire day.



Try corrective branches.



\### If carbohydrates are too high:



Consider:



\- Reducing rice

\- Reducing dosa/idli quantity

\- Increasing vegetables

\- Increasing protein accompaniment



\### If protein is too low:



Consider:



\- Increasing dal

\- Adding sundal

\- Adding curd

\- Adding paneer/tofu

\- Adding sprouts



\### If fat is too high:



Consider:



\- Reducing oil

\- Reducing coconut-based accompaniments

\- Reducing nuts

\- Choosing lower-fat protein options



\### If calories are too high:



Adjust portions before replacing the entire meal.



After adjustment, recalculate.
